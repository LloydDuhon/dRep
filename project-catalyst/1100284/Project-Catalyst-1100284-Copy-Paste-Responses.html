Delivered outcomes

Urban Farmer delivers a production farm-management application with Cardano wallet authentication, RESI-gated operations, farm and rack management, season and crop-event workflows, Cropchain Explorer, and season-level Merkle-root anchoring to Cardano metadata label 674. Farmers can manage planting, daily care, issues, loss, harvesting, packing, and traceability lot codes while preserving an append-only operational record stream.

Four of those guides—Radish Microgreens, Sunflower Microgreens, Butter Lettuce, and Amaranth Microgreens—were the accepted educational outputs for Milestone 4. The project also completed four recorded feedback sessions with farmers and non-farmers and used that feedback to improve demonstration clarity, pacing, audio, visuals, and instructional flow.

Critical aspects of success

The project produced a usable production application rather than a disconnected technical prototype. It retained the architecture accepted in the preproduction milestone: Season Beacon and Cash Register Aiken validators, wallet-signed authentication, RESI-gated actions, append-only Cropchain records, and Merkle-root anchoring to Cardano. This continuity reduced migration risk while moving the demonstrated workflow from preprod to mainnet.

The project also created a reviewer-verifiable evidence chain. Public transaction links, production configuration, endpoint snapshots, ordered record hashes, an independently recalculated Merkle root, the on-chain metadata, a public verifier result, and narrated walkthroughs allow the final system to be inspected without relying solely on project-team assertions.

Changes, challenges, and scope disclosure

The original proposal aspired to produce fifteen educational crop guides. Ten professionally produced long-form guides were completed and published. This was the only original aspiration not fully realized. The reviewed Statement of Milestones defined the accepted educational deliverable set, including the four guides required for the final production milestone, and those accepted milestone deliverables were completed.

The original proposal used broader Cosmos/IOHK sidechain language. By the approved preproduction release, the implemented evidence model was documented as Cropchain append-only records, Season Beacon and Cash Register Aiken validators, and Merkle-root anchoring to Cardano. That approved architecture was carried into production mainnet without a further architectural change.

Coordinating software development with real crop cycles and professional video production required substantially more time than the original four-month estimate. Wallet and indexer timing also required explicit recovery behavior because an on-chain transaction may confirm before an indexing provider reports it.

Lessons learned

  • Reviewer-verifiable evidence matters as much as implementation. Direct public proof makes complex infrastructure understandable and independently reviewable.
  • Batching operational records off-chain and anchoring a deterministic Merkle root at season close limits on-chain cost while preserving tamper-evident verification.
  • Submitted Cardano transactions must be persisted and reconciled idempotently so delayed indexer visibility does not create duplicate submissions.
  • Farmer and non-farmer feedback improved the usefulness and accessibility of the educational material.
  • Software, crop-growing, and media-production schedules require different planning rhythms.

Post-funding roadmap

The team will maintain the production service and Cardano integration, continue adding professionally produced guides for additional indoor crops, and focus product development on Living Greens classroom grow kits. The classroom work will allow older students to document and track their grows through Urban Farmer, supported by educator-friendly onboarding, permissions, and reporting.

The FDA states that the original Food Traceability Rule compliance date was January 20, 2026, that it proposed extending the date to July 20, 2028, and that Congress directed the agency not to enforce the rule before July 20, 2028. The additional preparation window will be used to improve traceability workflows and pilot classroom and farmer use. Urban Farmer is not represented as a compliance guarantee.

FDA source: https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/fsma-final-rule-requirements-additional-traceability-records-certain-foods
